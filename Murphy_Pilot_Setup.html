1. What you are turning on

Murphy Pilot is unattended Agentic trading. Agentic Trading (Grok Bot) is the brain: packs, research, name picks, process review. Grok.com is the hands: Robinhood snapshot, mechanical stops, and Agentic order: execution. The Operating Manual v7.1 is the rule book. This guide is the wiring. No per-trade approval. The owner watches the MurphyPilot desk. No money leaves Agentic for another account.

2. Robinhood

  • Confirm a primary individual account exists and is unrestricted. Agentic is a separate labeled book under that household, not a second login.
  • Desktop browser → https://grok.com/connectors → New Connector → Custom / Other / Bring Your Own MCP.
  • Paste https://agent.robinhood.com/mcp/trading and finish Robinhood OAuth. Grok never sees the password.
  • When prompted, open the Agentic account on desktop and complete disclosures.
  • Fund Agentic only. Pending deposits are instant and already in buying power — do not wait for ACH to settle and do not count pending twice. Deploy when BP is readable.
  • Agentic → Investing Settings → limited margin (reuse unsettled sale proceeds).
  • Learn disconnect on Agentic so you can halt without touching the primary account. Optional: enable broker fill pushes in addition to Grok trade emails.

3. Grok connectors and mail

  • Always allow place_equity_order and cancel tools at https://grok.com/connectors.
  • Connect Gmail. Trade, fault, pack, book-state, and process-review mail all go to justin.tucker.murphy@gmail.com from the job prompts.
  • Google Calendar is optional. It is not required for the pack calendar — that pack is built from official .gov sites and emailed.
  • Job notification method is OFF. Do not rely on the Automations product ping. The prompt mail is the bus.

5. Required lines in every trading prompt

  • Trade only Agentic. Ignore every other account. Never transfer money. Never print account numbers.
  • Skip per-trade review. Place on Agentic only. Fail closed on tool errors.
  • Liquid long-only stocks/ETFs, last ≥ $5. No options, crypto, shorts, inverse as core book.
  • Empty book with spendable buying power is a deploy, not an idle log.
  • 90/10. Slots = floor(equity / $75). New name only when unique names < slots.
  • 12-hour green lock per symbol. Same-symbol rebuy waits 24 hours. Loss proceeds → different name immediately.
  • Loss floor −5% / hard cap −6% anytime / flatten −10% / TIGHT −5%, each from that name’s own average cost.
  • Stall ladder per symbol, never portfolio: days 0–1 none; day 2 +5% from that name’s cost; later +4% from that name’s actual survive-mark each 2 business days.
  • HWM −20% freeze new buys, then TIGHT −10%. Restore NORMAL at +10% above trip.
  • No fourth same-name day-trade in five business days.
  • Bounce buys off the latest Agentic policy pack, world events, calendars, and announced events. Check anywhere reachable. Two-source rule for wires and social (X allowed). 2-session +5% must sit in the body of recent ATR. Stagger slots. Skip NFP/CPI/FOMC deploys unless that print is the concept.

6. First-day sequence after a rebuild

  • Confirm Agentic is readable and funded. Limited margin on.
  • Wait for the 06:30 Policy Pack (or run it once). Confirm an Agentic policy pack email arrived.
  • Next regular session after SuperGrok is available: snapshot watchdog pings Grok; Autopilot research emails Agentic order: if a slot is free.
  • 10:15 watch should stay quiet if AM deployed. If it mails empty-book fault, AM missed — treat as a process fail.
  • 11:50 / 13:50 should stay quiet if the snapshot shows ≥80% invested and no stop or stall is due.
  • 15:05 is the last chance to use idle cash that day.
  • Watch the desk at https://justintmurphy.github.io/MurphyPilot/ — asof and tape, not this chat.

7. How to read the House desk

Two charts. They are not the same clock.

  • Overall · last close — Truthifi 16:00 net worth. One print per weekday. Last close is Robinhood that afternoon plus Fidelity and Voya on their holdings date. Robinhood now in that card is a snapshot of live books, not a live chart.
  • Live equity · Robinhood session — every House snapshot of the four Robinhood books. This tape keeps the full print history (Aug 28 onward). Fidelity and Voya are not on this line.
  • Header on the phone shows the last print time and how stale it is, not a wall clock.
  • Watch asof and the tape. Do not wait on this chat for picks.

9. Halt

Disconnect the agent on Agentic, pause Agentic Trading routines, and pause grok.com email callables. Stop depositing. The agent cannot pull the primary account.

Sources for screens: Robinhood Agentic Trading overview; Trading with your agent; xAI Grok connectors. AI agents can misread markets or fail to run. The owner owns every fill and can lose the entire Agentic balance.
